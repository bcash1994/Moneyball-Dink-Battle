🏓💰 15 Player Pool Format

Format: 15 players are divided into two pools. Pool A has 8 players and Pool B has 7 players. Each pool plays pickleball doubles round robin games. The top 4 players from each pool advance to Round 2 for a total of 8 players. The top 4 players from Round 2 advance to the championship final. Rank 1 and Rank 2 become partners. Rank 3 and Rank 4 become partners. The championship is best of 3, and the first team to win 2 games is champion.
